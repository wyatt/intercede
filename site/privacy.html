Privacy policy

Intercede for Chrome · Last updated 16 November 2026

Intercede keeps everything it knows about you in your own browser. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends your data anywhere.

What it stores, and where

  • Your settings: the sites you track, their daily limits, your patron and timings. Stored with chrome.storage.sync, so if you use Chrome Sync they follow you to your other signed-in Chrome browsers, through Google's sync service like any extension setting.
  • Your usage: for each day, the time spent on each tracked site and how often your patron visited or you returned anyway. Stored with chrome.storage.local, on this device only. Sites you don't track are never recorded.

What it looks at

  • The address of your active tab, to tell whether it's a site you track and to count time on it.
  • A screenshot of the visible page, taken only when your patron arrives, so the page can break apart into pixels. It stays in that tab's memory for the animation and is discarded straight after; it is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Whether your screen is locked, so time doesn't count while you're away.

What it never does

  • Send any data off your device, to us or anyone else.
  • Sell, share or transfer your data, or use it for advertising or creditworthiness.
  • Read page contents beyond the momentary screenshot above, or record sites you haven't chosen to track.

Deleting your data

Remove a site in Intercede's settings to stop tracking it. Uninstalling Intercede deletes everything it stored.
